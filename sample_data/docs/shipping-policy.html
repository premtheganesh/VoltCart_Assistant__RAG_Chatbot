Shipping Policy

VoltCart is an online-only store, so every order is shipped to you from one of our warehouses in Reno, NV or Columbus, OH. This page explains your shipping options, what they cost, how long they take, and what to do if something goes wrong.

How fast will my order ship?

Orders are processed (picked and packed) within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 pm ET ship the next business day. Delivery times below are counted from the day your order ships, not the day you place it.

What shipping methods are available?

Method Delivery time Cost
Standard 3-5 business days Free on orders of $35 or more; $5.99 otherwise
Express 1-2 business days $14.99
Next-Day Next business day $24.99
Alaska and Hawaii (Standard only) 5-8 business days $12.99
Canada 7-12 business days $19.99 flat

What is the cut-off time for Next-Day shipping?

To get Next-Day delivery, place your order by 2 pm ET, Monday through Friday. Next-Day shipping is not available to Alaska, Hawaii or P.O. boxes.

Do you ship to Alaska and Hawaii?

Yes. Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by Standard shipping only, arrive in 5-8 business days, and cost $12.99. Express and Next-Day are not available to these states.

Can I ship to a P.O. box, APO or FPO address?

Yes. P.O. boxes, APO and FPO addresses can receive orders by Standard shipping only. Express and Next-Day options will not appear at checkout for these addresses.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to Canada only. Canadian orders take 7-12 business days and cost a flat $19.99. Duties and taxes are not included in that price; they are paid by the customer on delivery. We do not ship to any other countries at this time.

Which carriers do you use?

We ship with UPS, FedEx and USPS, depending on your address and the method you choose. As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking number. You can also follow your package from your account; see order tracking help for details.

What if my package is lost or arrives damaged?

We are sorry if that happens. Please report a lost or damaged package within 7 days of the delivery date, and we will send a replacement or issue a refund.

  1. Go to Account > Orders and find the order, or have your order number (for example, VC-10001) and email address ready.
  2. Contact our support team by chat, phone or email.
  3. For damaged items, share a short description and photos of the box and the product if you can.
  4. We will confirm the next step and arrange your replacement or refund.

If a package shows as delivered but you cannot find it, check around your entrance, with neighbors and with your building office before contacting us.

Can I pick up my order in a store?

No. VoltCart has no physical stores, so in-store pickup is not available. All orders are delivered by carrier.

Need more help?

Reach us through any of the channels on our contact and support hours page. If you need to return an item rather than report a shipping problem, see returns and refunds.
