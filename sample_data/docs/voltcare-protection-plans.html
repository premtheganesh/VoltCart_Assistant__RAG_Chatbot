VoltCare Protection Plans

Every VoltCart-brand product includes a 1-year limited warranty. VoltCare protection plans let you extend that coverage to 3 years total and, with VoltCare Plus, add protection against accidents.

What is the difference between VoltCare and VoltCare Plus?

Feature VoltCare VoltCare Plus
Coverage length 3 years total from delivery 3 years total from delivery
Mechanical and electrical failures Yes Yes
Battery capacity below 80% Yes Yes
Accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens) No Yes, up to 2 claims per 12 months
Express replacement (ships within 1 business day) No Yes
Deductible None Per accidental-damage claim (see below)

VoltCare extends coverage to 3 years total from your delivery date, covering mechanical and electrical failures and batteries that fall below 80% capacity.

VoltCare Plus includes everything in VoltCare, plus accidental damage such as drops, spills and cracked screens (up to 2 claims per 12 months), and express replacement that ships within 1 business day.

How much does VoltCare cost?

Plans are a one-time payment for a 3-year term. The price depends on the price of the product you are protecting.

Product price VoltCare VoltCare Plus
Under $100 $9 $19
$100-$299 $25 $45
$300-$699 $59 $99
$700-$1,199 $99 $169
$1,200 and up $149 $249

For example, the VoltBook Air 14 is priced at $899, so VoltCare costs $99 and VoltCare Plus costs $169. The VoltBook Pro 14 at $1,299 falls in the top tier: $149 for VoltCare or $249 for VoltCare Plus.

Is there a deductible?

Only VoltCare Plus has a deductible, and only for accidental-damage claims:

Product type Deductible per accidental-damage claim
Audio and smart home $29
Phones $49
Laptops $99

When can I buy a plan?

You can add VoltCare or VoltCare Plus at checkout or any time within 60 days of your product's delivery. After 60 days, a plan can no longer be added.

What is not eligible?

VoltCare plans are not available for accessories under $50 or for gift cards.

Can I cancel my plan?

Yes, you can cancel anytime.

  • Cancel within 30 days of purchasing the plan with no claims made, and you get a full refund.
  • After that, you receive a prorated refund.

To cancel, contact our support team with your order number. See contact and support hours for ways to reach us.

How do I make a claim?

Contact our support team with your order number and a description of the problem. Our team will confirm your coverage and walk you through the next steps.

Which plan is right for me?

If you mainly want peace of mind against defects and battery wear beyond the first year, VoltCare is a great fit. If your device travels with you every day, like a laptop, phone or earbuds, VoltCare Plus adds protection against drops and spills. For details on standard coverage, see our warranty policy.
