Order Tracking Help

Wondering where your order is? This page explains how to track it, what each order status means, and when you can still make changes.

What does my order number look like?

Every VoltCart order number starts with "VC-" followed by digits, for example VC-10001. You will find it in your order confirmation email and under Account > Orders. Have it ready whenever you contact support.

How do I track my order?

  1. Check your email. When your order ships, we email you a tracking number.
  2. Check your account. Sign in and go to Account > Orders to see the current status of each order.
  3. Use the carrier's site. We ship with UPS, FedEx and USPS. Enter your tracking number on the carrier's website for detailed scan updates.
  4. Ask our chat assistant. Our AI chat assistant is available 24/7 to help with order questions. Have your order number and email address ready.

If you checked out as a guest, use the tracking link in your shipping email, or contact support with your order number and email.

What does each order status mean?

Status What it means
Processing We have received your order and are preparing it. You can still cancel or change the address.
Shipped Your order has been handed to the carrier and your tracking number has been emailed. It can no longer be cancelled; you can refuse delivery or return it instead.
Out for delivery Your package is with the local driver and arriving today.
Delivered Your package has been delivered to the address.
Cancelled Your order was cancelled before shipping. Your refund or authorisation release takes 3-5 business days.
Return in progress You have started a return and we are waiting for the item to arrive.
Refunded We have inspected your return and issued your refund.

How long until my order ships?

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 pm ET ship the next business day. Delivery times depend on the method you chose; see our shipping policy.

Can I change my shipping address?

Yes, but only while your order is in Processing status. Go to Account > Orders, or contact support. Once an order has shipped, we can no longer change the address.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel only while the order is in Processing status, through Account > Orders or by contacting support. Your refund or authorisation release appears within 3-5 business days.

Once an order has Shipped, it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery or return the item after it arrives; see returns and refunds.

My tracking says delivered, but I don't have my package

First, check around your door, with neighbors and with your building's mail room. If you still cannot find it, report it within 7 days of the delivery date and we will send a replacement or a refund.

My tracking hasn't updated in a while

Carrier scans can pause while a package is in transit. If you are concerned, contact us through any channel on our contact and support hours page with your order number and we will check with the carrier.
