Payment and Financing

VoltCart offers several secure ways to pay, plus VoltPay financing on larger orders. Here is how payment works.

Which payment methods do you accept?

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • American Express
  • Discover
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • VoltCart gift cards (up to 2 gift cards per order)

Which payment methods are not accepted?

We do not accept cash on delivery, cryptocurrency or checks.

When will I be charged?

When you place your order, we place an authorisation on your card to confirm the funds are available. Your card is actually charged when the order ships. If you cancel while the order is still Processing, the authorisation is released within 3-5 business days.

What is VoltPay financing?

VoltPay lets you spread the cost of a larger purchase over monthly payments with 0% APR. VoltPay is subject to credit approval.

Order total VoltPay offer
$199-$498.99 0% APR for 6 months
$499 and up 0% APR for 12 months
Under $199 Not eligible

For example, a VoltBook Air 14 at $899 qualifies for 0% APR for 12 months.

Missed payments may incur fees according to the lender's terms, so please review the terms carefully before you apply.

How do I use VoltPay?

  1. Add your items to your cart and go to checkout.
  2. Choose VoltPay as your payment method.
  3. Complete the short credit application.
  4. If approved, review your payment plan and place your order.

How do VoltCart gift cards work?

You can pay with up to 2 VoltCart gift cards per order. Enter the gift card details at checkout. Gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned; see returns and refunds. VoltCare protection plans cannot be added to gift cards.

Do you charge sales tax?

Yes. Sales tax is charged according to the delivery state of your order. The exact amount is shown at checkout before you pay. For orders shipped to Canada, duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery; see our shipping policy.

Where does my refund go?

Refunds go back to your original payment method. See returns and refunds for timing.

Need help with a payment?

If a payment was declined or you see a charge you do not recognize, contact our team through any channel on our contact and support hours page. Have your order number ready, for example VC-10001.
