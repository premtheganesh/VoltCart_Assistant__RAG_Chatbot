Contact and Support Hours

Need a hand? VoltCart support is here to help with orders, returns, warranty claims and product questions. Choose the channel that works best for you.

How can I contact VoltCart?

Channel How to reach us Availability
AI chat assistant Chat on voltcart.example 24/7
Live chat with a human agent Chat on voltcart.example, then ask for an agent During human agent hours
Phone 1-800-555-0199 During human agent hours
Email support@voltcart.example Reply within 24 hours
Warranty claims support.voltcart.example or chat See our warranty policy

What are your support hours?

Our AI chat assistant is available 24/7, including weekends and holidays. It can answer policy questions and help you get started with common requests at any time.

Human agents are available by chat and phone during these hours:

Days Hours (Eastern Time)
Monday-Friday 8 am-8 pm ET
Saturday-Sunday 9 am-5 pm ET

Are you open on holidays?

Human agents are closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. The AI chat assistant remains available on those days, and you can still email us.

How quickly will you reply to my email?

We reply to emails sent to support@voltcart.example within 24 hours.

What should I have ready when I contact you?

To help us resolve your request quickly, please have:

  • Your order number, which looks like VC-10001
  • The email address used on the order
  • For warranty issues, the product's serial number and photos or video of the problem

How do I escalate an issue?

If you are not satisfied with the help you received, you can ask any agent for a supervisor. If you would like to file a complaint, we answer complaints within 2 business days.

  1. Ask the agent you are speaking with to connect you to a supervisor.
  2. Explain the issue and share your order number.
  3. If you submit a formal complaint, expect a response within 2 business days.

Can I visit a VoltCart store?

VoltCart is an online-only retailer, so there are no physical stores to visit and no in-store pickup. All support is provided by chat, phone and email.
