Warranty Policy

Every VoltCart-brand product, from VoltBook laptops and Nova phones to VoltSound earbuds and HomeHub smart home devices, comes with a 1-year limited warranty. This page explains what is covered, what is not, and how to file a claim.

How long does the warranty last?

The warranty lasts 1 year, starting on the delivery date of your product. If you want longer coverage or protection against accidents, take a look at our VoltCare protection plans.

What does the warranty cover?

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Examples include a laptop keyboard that stops registering keys, earbuds where one side stops producing sound, or a smart plug that no longer powers on.

Is the battery covered?

Yes. Batteries are covered if their capacity falls below 80% within the warranty period. Normal, gradual battery aging above that level is not a defect.

What is not covered?

  • Accidental damage, such as drops and cracked screens
  • Liquid damage (see the exception below)
  • Cosmetic wear, such as scratches and scuffs
  • Unauthorised repairs or modifications
  • Lost or stolen items

Is water damage ever covered?

Generally, liquid damage is not covered. The exception is products with an IP67 or IP68 rating: if you used the product within its rated limits, water ingress is covered. Check your product's specifications or manual for its rating and limits. Damage from use beyond the rated limits is not covered.

How do I file a warranty claim?

  1. Start a claim at support.voltcart.example or through chat.
  2. Provide your order number (for example, VC-10001), the product's serial number, a description of the problem, and photos or video showing the issue.
  3. Our team reviews your claim and, if approved, sends shipping instructions.
  4. Ship the item to us. We pay shipping both ways.
  5. We repair or replace your product and ship it back within 7-10 business days after we receive it.

Before filing, it can help to try the troubleshooting steps in your product manual, such as restarting or updating the device; many issues are resolved that way.

Can I transfer the warranty to someone else?

Yes. The warranty is transferable to a new owner. The new owner will need the original order number to make a claim. The coverage period still runs from the original delivery date.

Is the warranty the same as a return?

No. If you simply changed your mind, or the item arrived damaged, see returns and refunds and our shipping policy. The warranty is for defects that appear during normal use within the first year.

Questions?

If you are not sure whether an issue is covered, reach out through any channel on our contact and support hours page and our team will help.
