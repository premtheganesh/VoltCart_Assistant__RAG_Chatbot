General FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with VoltCart.

Do you have physical stores or in-store pickup?

No. VoltCart is an online-only retailer. We have no physical stores and do not offer in-store pickup. All orders ship from our warehouses in Reno, NV and Columbus, OH. See our shipping policy for delivery options.

Do you match prices?

Yes. We match a lower advertised price from Amazon (sold by Amazon), Best Buy, Walmart or Target for the identical, in-stock item. You can request a price match at the time of purchase or within 14 days after delivery.

Price matching does not apply to:

  • Marketplace sellers (including third-party sellers on Amazon)
  • Clearance items
  • Bundles
  • Black Friday doorbusters

To request a price match, contact support with your order number and a link to the competitor's listing.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes. Our VoltCampus program gives students 10% off laptops and audio with a verified .edu email address. Each student can use the discount on up to 1 laptop and 2 audio items per year.

Do you offer a military discount?

We do not offer a military discount at this time.

Can I trade in my old device?

Yes. We accept phones and laptops of any brand for trade-in in exchange for VoltCart credit.

  1. Get an instant quote online.
  2. We send you a free shipping kit.
  3. Pack and ship your device to us.
  4. Your VoltCart credit is issued within 5 business days of us receiving the device.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Gift wrapping is $4.99 per item, and you can add it at checkout. Gift receipts are also available.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes, VoltCart gift cards are available, and you can use up to 2 gift cards per order. Gift cards are final sale; see payment and financing for details.

Do you recycle old electronics?

Yes. We offer free mail-in recycling of old electronics, so you can dispose of old devices responsibly. Contact support to get started.

Which brands do you sell?

VoltCart sells its own brands: VoltBook laptops, VoltSound audio (Pulse, Stride and Echo), Nova phones, HomeHub, GlowBulb, GlowPlug and VoltView smart home devices, and VoltCharge and VoltSleeve accessories.

Are there companion apps for my devices?

Yes. Use the VoltSound app for audio products and the VoltHome app for smart home devices.

Do you ship outside the US?

We ship to Canada only. Canadian orders take 7-12 business days and cost $19.99 flat, with duties and taxes paid on delivery.
