# Returns and Refunds

**Effective November 1, 2026.** Changed your mind or received something that is not right? Most VoltCart purchases can be returned within **45 days of delivery**, and there is no restocking fee on any item. Here is everything you need to know.

## How long do I have to return an item?

You have **45 days from the delivery date** to return most items. The window starts on the day your order was delivered, not the day you placed it.

**Holiday returns:** purchases delivered between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## What condition does my return need to be in?

Opened items are welcome, as long as they are in like-new condition and include all original accessories and packaging. A few product types have extra requirements:

- **Earbuds and other in-ear products** must include all ear tips.
- **Laptops and phones** must be factory reset, with any account locks (such as Find My Device) removed.

## Is there a restocking fee?

No. VoltCart does not charge a restocking fee on any return, including opened laptops and phones.

## Who pays for return shipping?

- **Defective, damaged or wrong items:** we send you a free prepaid return label.
- **All other returns:** $7.99 is deducted from your refund to cover the return label.

## What can't be returned?

The following are final sale and cannot be returned:

- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final Sale" (clearance)
- Downloadable software

## How do I start a return?

1. Sign in and go to **Account > Orders**.
2. Find the order and select **Return item**.
3. Choose the item and the reason for the return.
4. Pack the item with all accessories and original packaging, attach the label, and drop it off with the carrier.

Checked out as a guest? Contact support with your order number (for example, VC-10001) and the email address used on the order, and we will start the return for you.

## How long do refunds take?

Once your return reaches us, we inspect it within **3 business days**. After inspection, your refund goes back to your original payment method within **5-10 business days**. Your order status changes to "Refunded" when the refund is issued.

## Can I exchange an item?

The fastest way is to return the item and place a new order. If your item is defective, you can instead request an exchange for the same item.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return opened headphones?

Yes. Opened headphones can be returned within 45 days of delivery if they are in like-new condition with all original accessories and packaging. For earbuds, all ear tips must be included. There is no restocking fee.

### Is there a restocking fee?

No. There is no restocking fee on any item, whether it is opened or unopened, and whatever the reason for the return.

### How long do refunds take?

We inspect returns within 3 business days of receiving them, then refund your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

### My item arrived broken. What should I do?

Report damage within 7 days of delivery, as described in our [shipping policy](/help/shipping-policy). For defects that show up later, see our [warranty policy](/help/warranty-policy).

### Can I return a laptop I opened and used for a week?

Yes, within 45 days of delivery, if it is in like-new condition, factory reset, and free of account locks. There is no restocking fee, but because it is a non-defect return, $7.99 return shipping is deducted from the refund.
