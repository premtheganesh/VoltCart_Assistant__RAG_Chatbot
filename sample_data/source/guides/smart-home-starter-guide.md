# Smart Home Starter Guide

You don't need a big budget or technical skills to start a smart home. You can begin with a couple of bulbs and add more over time. This guide covers five VoltCart products: the HomeHub Smart Speaker, HomeHub Mini, GlowBulb Color, GlowPlug Smart Plug and VoltView Video Doorbell. It explains how they work together and what to buy first.

## Smart Home Basics

- **Matter:** a shared standard that lets smart devices from different brands work with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and Apple Home. If a device supports Matter, you are not locked into one ecosystem.
- **Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz:** many smart devices connect straight to your home Wi-Fi. The 2.4 GHz band has longer range than 5 GHz, which is why bulbs and plugs often use only 2.4 GHz.
- **Thread:** a low-power mesh network for small devices such as sensors and locks. Each mains-powered Thread device can relay signals to the next, which extends coverage. A **Thread border router** connects that mesh to your home network.
- **Zigbee:** an older, widely used mesh standard for lights and sensors. Zigbee devices need a hub with a Zigbee radio.

## Do I Need a Hub?

**Not to get started.** The GlowBulb Color and GlowPlug Smart Plug connect directly to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi using Matter over Wi-Fi. You can control them from the VoltHome app with no hub. The VoltView Video Doorbell also connects directly to Wi-Fi.

A hub adds three things:

1. **Voice control.** The HomeHub and HomeHub Mini are smart speakers. During setup you choose either Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant as the voice assistant.
2. **Thread support.** Both hubs are Matter controllers and Thread border routers, so you can add Thread devices later.
3. **Zigbee support.** Only the full HomeHub Smart Speaker includes a Zigbee 3.0 radio. The HomeHub Mini has no Zigbee. Choose the HomeHub if you already own Zigbee lights or sensors from other brands.

The HomeHub also has a much bigger 360-degree speaker with a 3-inch woofer and four far-field microphones with a mute switch. The HomeHub Mini uses a 1.7-inch speaker. Choose the HomeHub if music matters to you.

## Setup Tips

- **Use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi for GlowBulb and GlowPlug.** They do not connect to 5 GHz. If your router combines both bands under one name and setup fails, temporarily enable a separate 2.4 GHz network. The VoltView works on both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz.
- **Place the hub centrally.** Put the HomeHub or HomeHub Mini in an open spot near where you use voice most, away from thick walls, microwaves and metal cabinets.
- **Check your doorbell's Wi-Fi signal.** Front doors are often far from the router. Test the signal at the door with your phone before mounting the VoltView, and move the router or add a mesh extender if the signal is weak.
- **Keep wall switches on.** Smart bulbs need constant power. If someone turns off the wall switch, the bulb goes offline.
- **Plugs indoors only.** Don't use the GlowPlug outdoors, and stay within its 15 A rating.
