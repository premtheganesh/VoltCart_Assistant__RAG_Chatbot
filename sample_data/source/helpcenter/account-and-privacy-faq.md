# Account and Privacy FAQ

Your account makes it easy to track orders and start returns, and your privacy matters to us. Here are answers to the most common account and privacy questions.

## Do I need an account to place an order?

No. Guest checkout is available, so an account is optional. With an account, you can view all your orders in one place under **Account > Orders**, track shipments and start returns yourself. Guest shoppers can still get help from support with their order number and email address.

## How do I reset my password?

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot password**.
3. Enter the email address on your account.
4. Follow the link in the email we send you to choose a new password.

## How do I delete my account?

1. Sign in and go to **Account > Settings > Privacy**.
2. Select **Delete account** and confirm.

Account deletion is processed within **30 days**. Please note that order records are kept for **7 years** because tax law requires it, even after your account is deleted.

## Can I download a copy of my data?

Yes. Go to **Account > Settings > Privacy > Export data**. We will email your data to you within **48 hours**.

## Does VoltCart sell my personal data?

No. VoltCart **never sells personal data**.

## How do I stop marketing emails?

Every marketing email we send includes an **unsubscribe link**. Click it to stop receiving marketing emails. You will still receive emails about your orders, such as shipping confirmations and tracking numbers.

## How long do you keep chat transcripts?

Chat transcripts are kept for **90 days** and then deleted.

## How do I update my shipping address on an order?

You can change the address on an order only while it is in Processing status. See [order tracking help](/help/order-tracking-help) for details.

## I think someone else accessed my account. What should I do?

Reset your password right away using **Forgot password** on the sign-in page, then contact our support team so we can help review your recent orders. You can find all our contact options on the [contact and support hours](/help/contact-and-support-hours) page.

## Who do I contact with a privacy question?

For any question not answered here, reach out to support@voltcart.example or use chat. Email replies are sent within 24 hours.
