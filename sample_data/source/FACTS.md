# VoltCart Fact Sheet (source of truth for writers — NOT ingested)

All knowledge documents must agree with this file and with `../data/catalog.json` (prices, specs, SKUs).

## Company
- VoltCart is an **online-only** US electronics retailer (no physical stores, no in-store pickup). Website: https://voltcart.example
- Sells its own brands: VoltBook (laptops), VoltSound audio (Pulse, Stride, Echo), Nova (phones), HomeHub / GlowBulb / GlowPlug / VoltView (smart home), VoltCharge / VoltSleeve (accessories).
- Companion apps: **VoltSound** (audio), **VoltHome** (smart home).
- Warehouses: Reno, NV and Columbus, OH.

## Shipping
- Orders are processed (packed) within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 pm ET ship the next business day.
- Standard: 3-5 business days. Free on orders of $35 or more; otherwise $5.99.
- Express: 1-2 business days, $14.99.
- Next-Day: $24.99, order by 2 pm ET Monday-Friday; not available to Alaska, Hawaii or P.O. boxes.
- Alaska and Hawaii: Standard only, 5-8 business days, $12.99.
- P.O. boxes / APO / FPO: Standard only.
- International: Canada only, 7-12 business days, $19.99 flat; duties and taxes paid by the customer on delivery. No other countries.
- Carriers: UPS, FedEx, USPS. Tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
- Lost/damaged packages: report within 7 days of the delivery date; we send a replacement or refund.

## Returns and refunds (current policy, v1)
- 30-day return window, counted from the **delivery** date.
- Opened items are accepted if in like-new condition with all original accessories and packaging.
- In-ear products (earbuds) must include all ear tips.
- Laptops and phones must be factory reset, with any account locks (e.g. Find My Device) removed.
- Restocking fee: 10% on **opened laptops and phones returned for non-defect reasons**. No restocking fee on other items, or on defective/wrong items.
- Return shipping: free prepaid label for defective, damaged or wrong items; otherwise $7.99 is deducted from the refund.
- Holiday returns: purchases delivered Nov 1 - Dec 24 can be returned until Jan 31.
- Final sale (not returnable): gift cards, items marked "Final Sale" (clearance), downloadable software.
- Refund timing: we inspect within 3 business days of receiving the return; refund to the original payment method in 5-10 business days after that.
- Exchanges: return the item and place a new order (fastest), or request an exchange for the same item (defective units only).
- How to start: Account > Orders > Return item, or ask support with order number and email.
- **v2 policy (TEST ONLY file, effective 2026-11-01):** window becomes **45 days**; restocking fee removed entirely; everything else unchanged.

## Warranty
- 1-year limited warranty on all VoltCart-brand products, starting on the delivery date.
- Covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- Batteries: covered if capacity falls below 80% within the warranty period.
- Not covered: accidental damage (drops, cracked screens), liquid damage, cosmetic wear, unauthorised repairs or modifications, lost or stolen items.
- Liquid exception: products with an IP67/IP68 rating used within their rated limits are covered for water ingress.
- Claim: support.voltcart.example or chat; provide order number, serial number, description and photos/video. Approved claims: repair or replacement shipped within 7-10 business days after we receive the item; we pay shipping both ways.
- The warranty is transferable to a new owner with the original order number.

## VoltCare protection plans
- **VoltCare**: extends coverage to 3 years total from delivery (mechanical and electrical failures, battery below 80%).
- **VoltCare Plus**: everything in VoltCare plus accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens), up to 2 claims per 12 months, express replacement (ships within 1 business day).
- Deductible (Plus only, per accidental-damage claim): $29 for audio and smart home, $49 for phones, $99 for laptops.
- Prices (one-time, 3-year term):

| Product price | VoltCare | VoltCare Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Under $100 | $9 | $19 |
| $100-$299 | $25 | $45 |
| $300-$699 | $59 | $99 |
| $700-$1,199 | $99 | $169 |
| $1,200 and up | $149 | $249 |

- Must be purchased within 60 days of the product's delivery. Cancel anytime for a prorated refund (full refund within 30 days of purchase if no claims).
- Not available for accessories under $50 or gift cards.

## Payment and financing
- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, VoltCart gift cards (up to 2 gift cards per order).
- Card is charged when the order ships (authorisation at checkout).
- **VoltPay financing** (subject to credit approval): 0% APR for 6 months on orders $199-$498.99; 0% APR for 12 months on orders $499 and up. Missed payments may incur fees per lender terms.
- Sales tax charged according to the delivery state.
- No cash on delivery, no cryptocurrency, no checks.

## Order statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | Order received and being prepared. Can still be cancelled or address changed. |
| Shipped | Handed to the carrier; tracking number emailed. Can no longer be cancelled (refuse delivery or return instead). |
| Out for delivery | With the local driver, arriving today. |
| Delivered | Delivered to the address. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled before shipping; refund/authorisation release in 3-5 business days. |
| Return in progress | Return started; waiting for the item to arrive. |
| Refunded | Return inspected and refund issued. |

- Change address or cancel: only while Processing (Account > Orders, or contact support).
- Order numbers look like `VC-10001`.

## Account and privacy
- Guest checkout allowed; account optional.
- Delete account: Account > Settings > Privacy > Delete account; processed within 30 days. Order records kept 7 years for tax law.
- Download your data: Account > Settings > Privacy > Export data (emailed within 48 hours).
- VoltCart never sells personal data. Marketing emails: unsubscribe link in every email.
- Chat transcripts are kept for 90 days, then deleted.
- Password reset via "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.

## Support
- AI chat assistant: 24/7.
- Human agents (chat and phone): Mon-Fri 8 am-8 pm ET, Sat-Sun 9 am-5 pm ET. Closed Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.
- Phone: 1-800-555-0199. Email: support@voltcart.example (reply within 24 hours).
- Escalation: ask any agent for a supervisor; complaints answered within 2 business days.

## General
- Price match: we match a lower advertised price from Amazon (sold by Amazon), Best Buy, Walmart or Target for the identical in-stock item, at purchase or within 14 days after delivery. Excludes marketplace sellers, clearance, bundles and Black Friday doorbusters.
- Student discount (VoltCampus): 10% off laptops and audio with a verified .edu email; max 1 laptop and 2 audio items per year.
- No military discount at this time.
- Trade-in: phones and laptops (any brand) for VoltCart credit; get an instant quote online, free shipping kit, credit issued within 5 business days of receipt.
- Gift wrapping: $4.99 per item. Gift receipts available.
- Recycling: free mail-in recycling of old electronics.
