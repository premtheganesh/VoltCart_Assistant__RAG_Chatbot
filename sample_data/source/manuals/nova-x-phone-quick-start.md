# Nova X Quick Start Guide

## Overview

The Nova X (SKU PH-NOVAX) is a flagship 6.7-inch smartphone with a triple rear camera including a 5x telephoto lens, a 5,000 mAh battery and 7 years of VoltOS and security updates. It supports 5G, eSIM and wireless charging, and is rated IP68. The Nova X is available in Obsidian, Glacier and Sage.

## What's in the box

- Nova X phone
- USB-C to USB-C cable (1 m)
- SIM eject tool
- Quick start card and safety information

A wall charger is not included. For the fastest charging, use a USB-C Power Delivery charger rated at 65 W or more.

## Getting started

1. Press and hold the power button on the right side for 3 seconds until the Nova logo appears.
2. If you use a physical SIM, insert the SIM eject tool into the hole on the bottom-left edge, place the nano-SIM in the tray with the gold contacts facing down, and push the tray back in.
3. Select your language and connect to Wi-Fi.
4. Sign in with your Google account to restore apps and data, or choose Set up as new.
5. Set up fingerprint unlock and a PIN.
6. Go to Settings, System, Software update and install any available updates.

## Activating an eSIM

The Nova X supports a nano-SIM and eSIM at the same time, and can store and keep up to 2 eSIMs active.

1. Request an eSIM from your carrier. You will receive a QR code or activation code.
2. Connect to Wi-Fi.
3. Go to Settings, Network and internet, SIMs, and tap Add SIM.
4. Select Download a SIM instead and tap Next.
5. Scan the QR code from your carrier. If you do not have a QR code, tap Enter it manually and type the activation code.
6. Tap Activate and wait for the download to finish.
7. Name the SIM, then choose which SIM to use for calls, texts and mobile data.

## Battery and charging

- Wired charging supports up to 65 W with a compatible USB-C Power Delivery charger. The VoltCharge 65W GaN Charger (AC-GAN65) is compatible.
- Wireless charging supports up to 15 W on a Qi-compatible pad.
- Reverse wireless charging: turn it on in Settings, Battery, then place earbuds or another Qi device on the back of the phone.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| eSIM download fails | Check your Wi-Fi connection and confirm the QR code has not already been used. Ask your carrier for a new code. |
| No mobile service | Check the selected SIM in Settings, Network and internet, SIMs, then restart. |
| Charging is slow | Use a 65 W or higher USB-C Power Delivery charger and the included cable. Make sure the USB-C port is clean and dry. |
| Phone is unresponsive | Hold the power and volume down buttons together for 12 seconds to force a restart. |

## IP68 care

The Nova X is rated IP68 for water and dust resistance. Water ingress is covered by the warranty when the phone is used within its rated limits.

- Rinse with fresh water after exposure to salt water, chlorine, soap or drinks, then dry.
- Do not charge the phone while the USB-C port is wet.
- Avoid hot water, steam and high-pressure water.
- Water resistance can decrease over time or after a drop.
