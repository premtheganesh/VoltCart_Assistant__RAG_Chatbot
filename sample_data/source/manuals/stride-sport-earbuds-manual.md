# Stride Sport Earbuds Manual

## Overview

The Stride Sport Earbuds (SKU AU-STRIDE) are true wireless sports earbuds with flexible ear hooks for a secure fit during runs and workouts. They are rated IP68 for dust and water resistance and include an Ambient mode that lets outside sound in, so you can stay aware of traffic and people around you. The earbuds are available in Volt Green and Black and work with the VoltSound app.

## What's in the box

- Stride Sport Earbuds (left and right)
- Charging case
- Ear tips in 3 sizes: S, M and L (M fitted)
- USB-C charging cable
- Quick start card

If you return the earbuds, please include all ear tips.

## Getting started

1. Place both earbuds in the case and charge it fully. The case LED glows amber while charging and green when full.
2. Open the case lid. The earbuds enter pairing mode automatically the first time and the case LED flashes white.
3. On your phone, open Bluetooth settings and select Stride Sport.
4. Install the VoltSound app and tap Add device.

The earbuds connect to one device at a time. To pair another device, open the case and hold the case button for 3 seconds until the LED flashes white.

## Fit guide

1. Start with the medium (M) ear tips that come fitted.
2. Hook the flexible ear hook over the top of your ear, then gently twist the earbud into your ear canal.
3. Bend the ear hook so it rests snugly against the back of your ear without pressing.
4. If the earbud moves or bass sounds thin, try the large (L) tips. If it feels uncomfortable, try the small (S) tips.
5. Use the Fit Test in the VoltSound app to check the seal in each ear.

## Using features

### Button controls

| Action | Left earbud | Right earbud |
|---|---|---|
| Press once | Turn Ambient mode on or off | Play or pause; answer or end a call |
| Press twice | Volume down | Next track |

You can change the button assignments in the VoltSound app.

### Ambient mode

Ambient mode lets outside sound in so you can hear traffic during outdoor runs. Press the left button once to turn it on or off. Set the Ambient level in the VoltSound app. The Stride Sport Earbuds do not have active noise cancelling.

### IP68 water and dust resistance explained

The IP68 rating means the earbuds are dust tight and can survive submersion in fresh water up to 1.5 m (about 5 ft) deep for up to 30 minutes. This protects them from sweat, rain and accidental drops into fresh water.

Please note:

- Bluetooth does not work underwater. Audio will stop as soon as the earbuds are submerged.
- The earbuds are not designed for swimming. Repeated submersion and water pressure from swimming strokes or diving are outside the rating.
- Avoid salt water, chlorinated pool water, soapy water and hot tubs. These can damage the seals and are not covered.
- The charging case is not water resistant. Never put wet earbuds in it.

After sweat or water exposure, rinse the earbuds with fresh water and let them air dry for at least 1 hour before charging. Water damage from use within the IP68 limits is covered by the warranty.

## Battery and charging

- Up to 9 hours of playback from the earbuds, plus 27 more hours from the case (36 hours total).
- Charge the case with the included USB-C cable.
- Battery levels are shown in the VoltSound app.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Only one earbud plays | Return both earbuds to the case for 10 seconds, then remove them. |
| Earbuds will not pair | Remove Stride Sport from your phone's Bluetooth list, then hold the case button for 3 seconds to re-enter pairing mode. |
| Earbuds fall out during runs | Try another ear tip size and adjust the ear hooks. |
| Audio stopped in the water | This is normal. Bluetooth does not work underwater. |
| Earbuds will not charge | Dry the earbuds and contacts, then reseat them in the case. |
| Need to factory reset | Place both earbuds in the case with the lid open, then hold the case button for 15 seconds until the LED flashes red. |

## Care and safety

- Do not use while swimming or in salt or chlorinated water.
