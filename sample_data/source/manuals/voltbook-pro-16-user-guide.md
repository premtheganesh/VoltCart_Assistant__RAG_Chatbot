# VoltBook Pro 16 User Guide

## Overview

The VoltBook Pro 16 (SKU VB-PRO16) is a creator laptop built for 4K video editing, color grading and 3D work. It pairs a factory-calibrated 16-inch mini-LED display with a 14-core VoltCore V9 processor, 32 GB of memory and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 graphics with 8 GB of video memory. It handles multi-track 4K timelines and 3D rendering smoothly. The VoltBook Pro 16 is available in Space Gray.

## Getting started

1. Connect the 140 W USB-C charger to either USB-C port. The status LED on the front edge glows amber while charging and white when full.
2. Open the lid. If the laptop does not turn on, press the power button for 2 seconds.
3. Choose your region, language and keyboard layout, then connect to Wi-Fi.
4. Sign in, then set up face login with the 1080p IR camera and create a backup PIN.
5. Install system updates, then open VoltBook Studio Center (preinstalled) and select Check for updates to get the latest GPU driver and display firmware.

## Display calibration and color profiles

Every VoltBook Pro 16 display is calibrated at the factory, and your calibration report card shows the measured results for your unit. You can switch color profiles in VoltBook Studio Center under Display, Color Profiles:

| Profile | Best for |
|---|---|
| DCI-P3 | Video editing and color grading for digital cinema and HDR delivery (covers 100% DCI-P3) |
| Display P3 | Content intended for phones, tablets and modern web browsers |
| sRGB | Web graphics and standard dynamic range video |
| HDR Video | Reviewing HDR footage, with peak brightness up to 1000 nits |
| Native | Full panel color gamut for general use |

Tips for accurate color:

- If you use a hardware colorimeter, select Native first, run your calibration software, and save the result as a Custom profile in VoltBook Studio Center.
- Select Restore factory calibration to return to the original settings at any time.

## Thermal and performance modes

Press Fn + P to cycle through performance modes, or select one in VoltBook Studio Center under Performance.

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Quiet | Lowest fan noise. Best for writing, browsing and meetings. |
| Balanced | Default mode. Good performance with moderate fan noise. |
| Performance | Maximum CPU and GPU power for 4K exports, 3D rendering and long renders. Fans run faster. Plug in the charger for full performance. |

## Using features

### 4K video editing workflow

- Import footage directly from camera cards using the UHS-II SD card reader on the right side.
- Enable GPU acceleration (NVIDIA RTX 5060) in your editing software for playback and exports.
- Connect a reference monitor using HDMI 2.1 or USB4.

### Installing a second SSD

Shut down the laptop, unplug the charger and remove the 10 Phillips screws on the bottom cover. Insert an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD into the slot marked SSD2, secure it with the screw provided, replace the cover and format the new drive. Damage caused during installation is not covered by the warranty.

## Battery and charging

- The 90 Wh battery delivers up to 12 hours of video playback. Heavy editing and rendering will use the battery much faster.
- Use the included 140 W USB-C charger. Lower-wattage chargers may not keep up with the laptop under load.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Exports are slower than expected | Select Performance mode, connect the 140 W charger and confirm GPU acceleration is enabled in your editing app. |
| Colors look different from another screen | Check which color profile is active and match it to your project's color space. |
| Fans are loud | Switch to Balanced or Quiet mode. |
| SD card not recognized | Insert the card label-side up until it clicks. |
| Laptop does not turn on | Charge for 15 minutes, then hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart. |

## Care and safety

- The laptop is not water resistant. Keep liquids away.
- Do not block the vents on the bottom and rear edge.
- Before returning the laptop, factory reset it and remove account locks such as Find My Device.
