# GlowBulb Color and GlowPlug Smart Plug Guide

## Overview

This guide covers two Matter over Wi-Fi products that need no hub.

- **GlowBulb Color (2-pack)**, SKU SH-GLOWBULB: A19 color smart bulbs with an E26 base, 16 million colors and tunable white.
- **GlowPlug Smart Plug (2-pack)**, SKU SH-GLOWPLUG: Matter smart plugs with energy monitoring, ideal for scheduling lamps, fans and coffee makers.

Both products work with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and Apple Home (via Matter), and are set up with the VoltHome app.

## Wi-Fi requirement: 2.4 GHz only

GlowBulb and GlowPlug connect only to **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi networks. They cannot connect to 5 GHz networks. 

- If your router uses separate network names for each band (for example, HomeWiFi and HomeWiFi-5G), connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz network during setup.
- If your router combines both bands under one name, setup usually works. If it fails, temporarily turn off the 5 GHz band in your router settings, or enable a separate 2.4 GHz network.

## Pairing with the VoltHome app

1. Install the VoltHome app and connect your phone to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Turn on Bluetooth.
2. Screw the GlowBulb into a lamp or fixture and switch it on, or plug the GlowPlug into an indoor outlet.
3. The device enters pairing mode automatically on first power-up. The bulb slowly pulses warm white, and the plug's LED flashes blue.
4. In VoltHome, tap Add, then Add device, then scan the Matter QR code.
5. Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password.
6. When pairing completes, the bulb flashes green twice and the plug LED turns solid blue.
7. Assign the device to a room and give it a name, such as Bedroom Lamp.

To add the device to Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home, open the device in VoltHome, select Settings, Matter, Share with another app, and scan the new code in that app.

## Using features

### GlowBulb lighting

- Choose from 16 million colors, or tunable white from 2,200 K to 6,500 K.
- Create Scenes and wake-up or wind-down schedules.
- Leave the wall switch on, or the bulb cannot be controlled from the app.

### GlowPlug control and schedules

- Press the button on the side of the plug to turn it on or off manually.
- Set schedules, timers and Away mode.
- Do not exceed 15 A. Indoor use only.

### Energy monitoring (GlowPlug only)

GlowPlug measures the power used by whatever is plugged into it. In VoltHome, open the plug and tap Energy to see:

- Current power draw in watts
- Daily, weekly and monthly energy use in kilowatt-hours (kWh)
- Estimated cost, based on your electricity rate

## Factory reset

| Device | How to reset | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| GlowBulb Color | Switch the light off and on 5 times, with about 1 second on and 1 second off each time, ending with the light on. | Bulb flashes red three times, then pulses warm white (pairing mode). |
| GlowPlug Smart Plug | Press and hold the side button for 10 seconds. | LED flashes red, then flashes blue (pairing mode). |

After a reset, remove the device from VoltHome and other apps, then pair it again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Device not found during setup | Make sure your phone is on the 2.4 GHz network and Bluetooth is on. Factory reset the device and try again. |
| Setup fails at the Wi-Fi step | The devices do not connect to 5 GHz. Use a 2.4 GHz network or temporarily disable 5 GHz on your router. Check the password. |
| Device shows as offline | Check that the wall switch is on (bulb) or the outlet has power (plug). Restart your router. |
| Bulb flickers | Do not use GlowBulb with dimmer switches. Use a standard on/off switch. |

## Care and safety

- GlowBulb: turn off power and let the bulb cool before changing it. Do not use with dimmer switches.
- GlowPlug: indoor use only, rated 15 A, 120 V. Do not daisy-chain plugs.
