# HomeHub Smart Speaker Setup Guide

## Overview

The HomeHub Smart Speaker (SKU SH-HOMEHUB) is a smart speaker and smart-home hub in one. It has a 360-degree speaker with a 3-inch woofer, and built-in radios that act as a Matter controller, a Thread border router and a Zigbee 3.0 hub. During setup you choose either Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant as your voice assistant. HomeHub works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and Apple Home (via Matter). It is available in Charcoal and Chalk. This guide also covers the HomeHub Mini (SKU SH-HOMEHUBMINI).

## Getting started

Before you begin, install the VoltHome app (iOS or Android) and make sure your phone is connected to your home Wi-Fi network and has Bluetooth turned on.

1. Place HomeHub in a central location and connect the wall adapter to HomeHub and plug it into an outlet. The light ring on top glows white while starting up, then pulses orange when ready for setup.
2. Open the VoltHome app and tap Add, then Add device, then HomeHub. The app finds HomeHub using Bluetooth.
3. Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password.
4. Choose your voice assistant: Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. Sign in to your Amazon or Google account when prompted.
5. Assign HomeHub to a room and wait while it installs updates. The light ring spins blue during updates and turns off when setup is complete.

### Changing your voice assistant later

To switch assistants, open VoltHome, select HomeHub, then Settings, Voice Assistant, Change assistant. Your paired devices stay connected.

## Matter, Thread and Zigbee explained

- **Matter** is a common smart-home standard supported by Amazon, Google and Apple. As a Matter controller, HomeHub can add and control Matter devices, and a Matter device can be shared with more than one ecosystem at the same time.
- **Thread** is a low-power wireless mesh network used by many Matter sensors, locks and bulbs. As a Thread border router, HomeHub connects Thread devices to your Wi-Fi network and the internet.
- **Zigbee** is an established low-power mesh standard used by many existing bulbs, sensors and switches. HomeHub includes a Zigbee 3.0 radio, so you can control these devices without a separate hub.

Wi-Fi Matter devices such as GlowBulb Color and GlowPlug do not need a hub, but HomeHub can still control them.

## Adding devices

1. Put the device in pairing mode by following its instructions.
2. In VoltHome, tap Add, then Add device.
3. For a Matter device, scan the Matter QR code on the device or its packaging. For a Zigbee device, select Zigbee device and wait for HomeHub to find it.
4. Assign the device to a room and give it a name.

## Using HomeHub with Apple Home via Matter

HomeHub works with Apple Home through Matter.

1. In VoltHome, select HomeHub, then Settings, Matter, Share with another app.
2. VoltHome shows a new pairing code.
3. On your iPhone, open the Home app, tap Add Accessory, and scan the code or enter it manually.

## Using features

- Microphone mute: slide the mute switch on the back. The light ring glows red while the microphones are off.

## HomeHub Mini differences

The HomeHub Mini is a smaller, lower-cost version. It has a 1.7-inch full-range speaker instead of a 3-inch woofer, is powered by an included USB-C adapter, and comes in Charcoal, Chalk and Sky. It includes a Matter controller and Thread border router but **does not have a Zigbee radio**, so Zigbee devices require the full HomeHub.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| VoltHome cannot find HomeHub | Turn on Bluetooth and location permission for VoltHome, and keep your phone within 10 ft (3 m). |
| Zigbee device not found | Reset the device and keep it within 15 ft (4.5 m) of HomeHub during pairing. The HomeHub Mini does not support Zigbee. |
| Voice assistant does not respond | Check that the microphones are not muted (red light ring). |
| Need to factory reset | Hold the + and - buttons together for 15 seconds until the light ring flashes orange. |

## Care and safety

- Indoor use only. Keep away from water and heat.
- Clean with a dry, lint-free cloth.
