# VoltBook Gamer 15 User Guide

## Overview

The VoltBook Gamer 15 (SKU VB-GAMER15) is a gaming laptop with a 15.6-inch QHD 240 Hz display, a 16-core VoltCore V9H processor and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 graphics with 8 GB of video memory. Three cooling modes let you choose between quiet operation and maximum frame rates, and the per-key RGB keyboard can be customized to match your setup. The VoltBook Gamer 15 comes in Black.

## Getting started

1. Connect the 240 W barrel charger to the round power port on the rear of the laptop, then plug it into a wall outlet. The power LED on the front edge glows amber while charging and white when full.
2. Open the lid and press the power button above the keyboard for 2 seconds.
3. Follow the on-screen setup to select your region and language, connect to Wi-Fi (or plug in an Ethernet cable to the 2.5 GbE port) and sign in.
4. Install system updates, then open VoltBook Command Center (preinstalled) and select Updates to get the latest GPU driver and firmware.

## Using features

### Cooling modes

Press Fn + F (or use Command Center, Performance) to cycle through three cooling modes. The power button ring changes color to show the active mode.

| Mode | Power button color | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Silent | Blue | Browsing, video and light games. Lowest fan noise and longest battery life. |
| Balanced | White | Everyday gaming with moderate fan noise. Default mode. |
| Turbo | Red | Maximum CPU and GPU performance for demanding games. Fans run at high speed. Turbo is only available when the 240 W charger is connected. |

If you unplug the charger while in Turbo, the laptop switches to Balanced automatically.

### Display

The 240 Hz QHD panel with a 3 ms response time gives smooth, sharp motion in fast games. Connect an external monitor using HDMI 2.1 or USB-C (USB4).

### RGB keyboard lighting

Every key has its own RGB backlight. In Command Center, open Lighting to choose effects such as Static, Breathing, Wave, Reactive and Off, set per-key colors, and save up to 5 profiles. Press Fn + Space to adjust brightness through Off, Low, Medium and High. Press Fn + Right Arrow to switch between saved lighting profiles.

### Upgrading memory (RAM)

The laptop ships with 16 GB of DDR5 memory in two slots and can be upgraded to a maximum of 64 GB (two 32 GB modules). Use matching DDR5 SO-DIMM modules for dual-channel performance.

1. Shut down the laptop completely and disconnect the charger.
2. Turn the laptop over and remove the 11 Phillips screws on the bottom cover.
3. Lift the cover gently from the rear vents.
4. Release the clips on each side of the memory slot and remove the existing module.
5. Insert the new module at a 30-degree angle and press it down until the clips lock.
6. Replace the cover and screws.

Damage caused during an upgrade is not covered by the warranty.

## Battery and charging

- The 80 Wh battery provides up to 6 hours of light use, such as browsing and documents. Gaming on battery will drain it much faster and reduces performance.
- For full gaming performance and Turbo mode, always connect the 240 W barrel charger.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Low frame rates | Connect the 240 W charger and select Turbo mode. |
| Turbo mode is grayed out | Turbo requires the 240 W charger. Check that the barrel plug is fully inserted. |
| Fans are very loud | Switch to Silent or Balanced. Clean dust from the vents with compressed air. |
| New RAM not detected | Reseat the modules until both clips lock, and use DDR5 SO-DIMM modules. |
| Laptop will not turn on | Connect the charger, then hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart. |

## Care and safety

- Use the laptop on a hard surface so air can flow freely through the bottom and rear vents.
- The laptop is not water resistant. Keep drinks away from the keyboard.
- Before returning the laptop, factory reset it and remove account locks such as Find My Device.
