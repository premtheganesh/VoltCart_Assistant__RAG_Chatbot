# VoltBook Air 14 User Guide

## Overview

The VoltBook Air 14 (SKU VB-AIR14) is a thin-and-light 14-inch laptop with a 2.8K OLED display and all-day battery life. It is designed for students, travel and office work. At 1.2 kg (2.6 lb) it fits easily in a backpack, and its 60 Wh battery delivers up to 16 hours of video playback on a single charge. The VoltBook Air 14 is available in Silver and Midnight.

## Getting started

1. Connect the 65 W USB-C charger to either USB-C port on the left side of the laptop. The charging LED next to the ports glows amber while charging and turns white when the battery is full.
2. Open the lid. The laptop powers on automatically the first time. If it does not, press the power button in the top-right corner of the keyboard for 2 seconds.
3. Follow the on-screen setup to choose your region, language and keyboard layout.
4. Connect to a Wi-Fi network and sign in with or create your user account.
5. Install the latest updates. The laptop may restart more than once.
6. Open the VoltBook Control Center app (preinstalled) to register your device and check for firmware updates.

## Using features

### Display

The 2.8K OLED panel offers deep blacks and vivid colors. You can adjust brightness with the F5 and F6 keys. In VoltBook Control Center, open Display and choose between Vivid, Natural and Reading color modes.

### Ports and external displays

Both USB-C ports support USB4, charging and display output, so you can charge from either port and connect a USB-C monitor or dock. The USB-A 3.2 port is suitable for flash drives, mice and other accessories. Use the 3.5 mm jack for wired headphones or a headset.

### Webcam and privacy shutter

The 1080p webcam has a physical privacy shutter above the display. Slide the shutter to the right to cover the lens. When the shutter is closed, the camera shows a black image in video apps.

### Power modes

In VoltBook Control Center, open Power and choose Battery Saver, Balanced or Performance. Balanced is recommended for everyday use. Battery Saver lowers screen brightness and limits background activity to extend battery life.

## Battery and charging

- The 60 Wh battery provides up to 16 hours of video playback. Actual run time depends on brightness, apps and wireless use.
- Use the included 65 W USB-C charger for the fastest charging. The VoltCharge 65W GaN Charger (AC-GAN65) is also compatible.
- To extend long-term battery health, turn on Battery Care in VoltBook Control Center under Power. This limits charging to 80% when the laptop is plugged in most of the time.
- The battery is covered by warranty if its capacity falls below 80% within the warranty period.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Laptop does not turn on | Connect the charger for at least 15 minutes, then press the power button for 2 seconds. If there is still no response, hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart. |
| Charging LED does not light | Try the other USB-C port and check that the cable is fully inserted. Use the included 65 W charger. |
| External monitor not detected | Connect the monitor to a USB-C port (the USB-A port does not support video). Check that the monitor input is set correctly. |

To restore the laptop to factory settings, go to Settings, System, Recovery and choose Reset this PC. Back up your files first. Before returning a laptop, you must factory reset it and remove any account locks such as Find My Device.

## Care and safety

- Clean the display with a soft, dry microfiber cloth. Do not spray liquid directly on the screen.
- The VoltBook Air 14 is not water resistant. Keep drinks away from the keyboard.
- Do not block the air vents on the underside when using the laptop on soft surfaces.
- Use a padded case such as the VoltSleeve 14 Laptop Sleeve (AC-SLEEVE14) when traveling.
