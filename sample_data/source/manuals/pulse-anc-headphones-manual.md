# Pulse ANC Headphones Manual

## Overview

The Pulse ANC Headphones (SKU AU-PULSEANC) are over-ear, closed-back wireless headphones with adaptive noise cancelling, up to 40 hours of battery life with ANC on and multipoint Bluetooth that connects to two devices at once. They fold flat for travel and are available in Black and Sand.

- 3.5 mm audio cable
- Quick start card

## Controls

The controls are on the right ear cup.

- Power button: press and hold for 2 seconds to turn on or off. Hold for 5 seconds to enter pairing mode.
- ANC button: press to cycle through Max ANC, Adaptive ANC and Transparency.
- Touch panel: tap twice to play or pause; swipe forward or back to skip tracks; swipe up or down to change volume; tap twice during a call to answer or end it.
- Status LED: flashes blue and white in pairing mode, glows solid white for 3 seconds when connected, flashes red when the battery is low and glows amber while charging.

## Getting started

1. Charge the headphones fully, then press and hold the power button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue and white. You will hear "Pairing."
2. On your phone, open Bluetooth settings and select Pulse ANC.
3. Install the VoltSound app, tap Add device and install any firmware update.

## Using features

### Noise-cancelling modes

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| Max ANC | Strongest noise reduction for flights, trains and busy offices. |
| Adaptive ANC | Adjusts noise cancelling to your surroundings (default). |
| Transparency | Lets outside sound in so you can hear announcements and conversations without removing the headphones. |

To turn ANC off for up to 55 hours of playback, select Off on the Noise Control screen in the VoltSound app.

### Multipoint pairing with two devices

The headphones can stay connected to two devices at once, such as a laptop and a phone. Audio plays from the active device, and calls take priority.

1. Pair the first device as described in Getting started.
2. Turn off Bluetooth on the first device temporarily.
3. Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue and white.
4. On the second device, open Bluetooth settings and select Pulse ANC.
5. Turn Bluetooth back on for the first device and select Pulse ANC.
6. In the VoltSound app, check Connections. Both devices should show as Connected.

The headphones connect to 2 devices at once, and multipoint is on by default. LDAC is available with one device at a time; when two devices are connected, the headphones use AAC or SBC.

### VoltSound app

- Noise Control: choose a noise-cancelling mode.
- Equalizer: choose a preset or create a custom 5-band EQ.
- Connections: view, connect or remove paired devices and turn multipoint on or off.
- Settings: firmware updates and factory reset.

### Wired listening

Connect the 3.5 mm cable to the port on the left ear cup to listen with a wired connection. Wired listening works even when the battery is empty, although ANC requires battery power.

## Battery and charging

- Up to 40 hours of playback with ANC on and 55 hours with ANC off.
- Fast charge: 10 minutes of charging gives 5 hours of playback.
- A full charge takes about 2 hours.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Headphones will not pair | Hold the power button for 5 seconds to enter pairing mode. Remove Pulse ANC from your device's Bluetooth list and pair again. |
| Second device will not connect | Check that multipoint is on in VoltSound under Connections. Only 2 devices can be connected at once, so disconnect a third device. |
| Noise cancelling seems weak | Select Max ANC and make sure the cushions seal around your ears. |
| Audio cuts out | Stay within 33 ft (10 m) of the device. Select AAC instead of LDAC in the app. |
| Need to factory reset | Turn the headphones on, then hold the power and ANC buttons together for 15 seconds until the LED flashes red three times. |

## Care and safety

- The headphones are not water resistant. Keep them away from rain and liquids.
- Wipe the ear cushions with a soft, dry cloth.
- Store the headphones folded in the carrying case.
